- 1:00-3:00: Explain why hub-and-spoke plus edge buffering fits 23 intermittently connected stores and a four-person IT team.
- 3:00-5:00: Walk from an offline customer record through provisional ID, MDM matching, stewardship, and propagation.
- 5:00-7:00: Show one raw defect, the exact cleaning rule, before/after metric, and what the metric cannot prove.
- 7:00-9:00: Trace monthly active customers from source through warehouse to dashboard; defend the KPI definition.
- 9:00-11:00: Explain breach escalation, Uganda DPPA duties, GDPR partner benchmark, and why legal applicability must be assessed.
- 11:00-13:00: Defend the UGX 480M arithmetic, month-6 quick wins, and what stops if the budget halves.
- 13:00-15:00: Red-team critique, personal reflection, and one decision you would change with better evidence.

## Likely challenge questions

**What if the budget is halved?** Protect endpoint encryption/MDM, breach response, data ownership, a narrow batch warehouse, and one pilot store cluster. Defer streaming, commercial catalog software, and regional expansion. Re-baseline the 18-month outcome and obtain Board approval; do not silently claim full scope.

**Find a flaw in your ETL.** The demo uses fixed planning FX rates and source channel as a source-system label. Production needs dated finance-approved FX, a source registry independent of channel, and a historical replay test. The error log is synthetic because the supplied log was absent.

**Why not auto-merge more records?** False merges can combine loyalty history and expose one person's data to another. Auto-link only exact high-confidence evidence with no protected-attribute conflict, make merges reversible, and send ambiguous pairs to a steward.

**What does the 0% after-dedup result mean?** It means no rows collide under this exact deterministic demo key after cleansing. It does not prove there are no real-world duplicate people; the original instructor dataset, match review, and production identifiers are needed.

**Why not stream everything?** Inventory availability and some payment-risk signals may benefit from low-latency events, but monthly Board reporting, customer segmentation, and supplier reconciliation can use scheduled batch. Streaming increases operational cost and support burden for this small team.

**What would change your mind?** A measured outage/retry study, false-merge sample review, source freshness audit, PCI/payment processor contract constraints, and a total-cost-of-ownership estimate from a pilot.
