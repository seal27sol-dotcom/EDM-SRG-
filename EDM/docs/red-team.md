# D2 | Red-Team Critique

The strongest case against this strategy is that it could confuse an attractive target diagram with a deliverable operating capability. SRG has four IT staff, only two who write SQL, no data-engineering experience, poor connectivity, and an 18-month deadline. The proposal introduces a hub, MDM, warehouse, quality gates, role governance, a catalog, endpoint controls, and selected event processing. Even if each piece is individually sensible, integration and change management can exceed the capacity of the people who must keep stores selling. A managed-service invoice does not transfer accountability; it can create vendor lock-in, opaque outages, and cost escalation. The UGX 480M budget is a cap without vendor quotes, tax treatment, exchange-rate risk, or a validated workload estimate. The 17.7% contingency could be consumed by connectivity remediation or licensing before the data platform is stable.

The technical design also relies on a simplifying assumption that central ownership improves data. It may instead centralize bad definitions and slow stores. During an outage, a cashier will create a local customer regardless of the MDM policy if the alternative is refusing a sale. An encrypted outbox and provisional ID address the transaction path but not necessarily conflict resolution, duplicate loyalty accounts, or a delayed consent withdrawal. A re-merge could still assign points to the wrong person. Even exact phone matching is not proof of identity: Ugandan households may share phones, phone numbers are recycled, and a contact may be entered by a cashier without verification. A weighted score is not objective merely because it is numeric; field weights encode judgments, and district/name signals can create uneven error rates across language groups.

The proposal's strongest evidence is synthetic. The brief's actual customer, product, and sales files were not present, so the 22.62% duplicate measurement, 4.92% product SKU collision, 60,000-row processing, dashboard results, ETL error log, and HR records are reproducible demonstrations, not SRG findings. The generated 8.4% stock variance cause is not established at all: there are no time-aligned book balances, physical counts, transfers, goods receipts, returns, or adjustment approvals. A Board member could reasonably reject any claim that this portfolio proves the business case. The correct response is to preserve the distinction, obtain the instructor files, rerun every script, and gather operational evidence before a production commitment. The demo should be graded for method and transparently labeled, not submitted as measured company truth.

The security section could also overstate legal confidence. The Jinja breach demands urgent response, but DPPA section wording, regulations, PDPO directions, contract duties, and cross-border arrangements must be checked against current primary sources by competent counsel. GDPR does not automatically apply because an EU distributor asks for equivalent practices. Treating the 72-hour GDPR clock as Uganda's statutory clock would be wrong. Conversely, treating the EU requirement as only a contract checkbox could miss territorial scope or processor obligations. The proposed DPIA cannot close this gap without a real data-flow map, app design, subprocessors, retention facts, and a legal basis per purpose.

The scope is also too broad for the assignment's one-week schedule. A polished static dashboard does not satisfy the stated Power BI or Tableau Public criterion unless the lecturer accepts a different host. No external hosting account was available, so there is no public URL or live-host screenshot. The PostgreSQL GRANT test is supplied but has not been executed against a provisioned test database; source templates and the instructor ETL log were absent. The essay's generic career narrative must be changed to the student's real path, and AI assistance must be declared under KIU rules. Without that work, a complete-looking repository could fail both the rubric and academic integrity expectations.

The rebuttal is not to claim that these weaknesses disappear. It is to stage the work and permit a no-go decision. First, apply the low-regret endpoint, access, breach-escalation, and ownership controls that address observed risk. Second, baseline real data and store connectivity with a limited pilot. Third, require total-cost, data-quality, match-precision, replay, and user-adoption evidence before MDM or warehouse expansion. Fourth, defer stream processing and catalog licensing until an owner and an economically justified use case exist. If the pilot cannot meet the 99.5% reconciliation gate or store staff cannot operate the offline process, do not scale it; simplify the architecture or change the process. If the Board halves funding, retain breach prevention, essential stewardship, and a small reconciled batch dashboard, while explicitly dropping regional rollout, broad MDM propagation, and streaming. The strategy's value is its decision gates and honest limits, not a promise that all 18-month outcomes are guaranteed.

The attack therefore wins on two points today: the numerical business case is not yet empirical, and the platform/hosting choice does not automatically meet the assignment's named BI requirement. It does not invalidate the direction, but it means the next approval should be for a short discovery and control phase, not an irreversible platform purchase.
